// the gap

Tools adopted is not work redesigned

99%
  • Ramp hit 99% AI-tool adoption. Most people were still stuck, using the models like a faster autocomplete.
  • "Driving a Ferrari with the handbrake on."
  • The teams pulling away didn't buy different AI. They changed the shape of the work.
  • The whole shift is that gap. Tools adopted vs. work redesigned.

// act 01 · the factory

Close the loop

  • One system. Ideate, prioritize, execute, evaluate, iterate.
  • Today it's a pipeline. Hand-offs between roles, weeks between stages.
  • The shift isn't "go faster." It's closing the loop so it turns in days, not quarters.
  • First-principles question. Not "how do we go faster," but "is the process even shaped right."
  • The human runs the whole loop, not one node. That's orchestration, product's real job.
  • Spec writer to strategic curator. A background agent is the primitive you wire stages with.
SourceRamp Labs, The MAD Podcast·youtube.com/watch?v=Fgzs4FBmRYw

// act 02 · shared team operating systems

The asset is the shared model of the problem

team-os/ .claude/ # commands, skills product/ # PRDs, customer notes team/ # onboarding, retros
"Not rolled out until the repository is updated."
  • Individual AI use makes one person faster. Teams that change share their AI infrastructure.
  • One PM supports 20 through a shared, version-controlled repo.
  • Working rule, a feature isn't shipped until the repo is updated. The system learns from every release.
  • A partner who'd never opened GitHub now ships daily. Shared context, not coding ability, makes the tools usable.
  • The durable asset is the model of the problem, not the app. It's what lets you regenerate the app.
SourceHannah Stahlberg, "How I AI"·youtube.com/watch?v=0UArKLQ6bXA

// act 03 · what it means for the people in the room
The task may change. The people don't.
Ownership doesn't move.
  • If anyone can generate a PRD, the PRD was never the job. The mission is unchanged. Help the team understand what to build and why.
  • Volume is free now. Taste is the bottleneck. Judgment and taste are what concentrate.
  • The AI is a brilliant junior who read every document but wasn't in the hallway after the leadership sync. Reading the room is still a human's job.
  • What survives and grows. Judgment, problem framing, owning the outcome. The tasks that crowded them out are receding.
SourceAparna Dhinakaran, "How I AI"·youtube.com/watch?v=DL-pUGcfrf4
