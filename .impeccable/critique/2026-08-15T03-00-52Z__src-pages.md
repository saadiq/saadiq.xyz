## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored identity, un-authored structure. The `//` gold mono labels, `_`-suffixed metadata keys, proof_/results_ field labels, Instrument Serif at 72px over near-black, and the status bar are genuinely specific to this product. But the specificity is surface treatment only: one layout repeated ~30 times across five pages (left column, gold label, serif heading, muted paragraph, bare text link). Terminal metaphor asserted through decoration, never behavior. The design contradicts what it sells: sells one human's judgment with no portrait (the /about bio grid reserves an empty div where one belongs); sells proof while setting proof_ in the smallest dimmest type and giving the largest type to anonymized descriptors; positions as "demonstrates live, not slideware" with zero img/svg/video/picture elements in the source tree.

Deterministic scan: static scan 1 finding (Inter via Google Fonts in BaseLayout.astro:19; CLI snippet mislabeled it "instrument serif"). Rendered-DOM: 33 desktop / 28 mobile findings across 8 rules. Independent agreement between assessments: 10px stat-source attributions (~3.3:1 contrast, fails 4.5:1); missing prefers-reduced-motion guard (nothing in src/); 20 em-dashes on /work (violates the site's own vault writing rules). Detector-only catches: ~139-char line lengths on three homepage paragraphs; 8 heading-rhythm violations (homepage "Why not them" H2s + all six /speaking cards bind to the block above). False positives: all 6 text-occlusion hits are the detector measuring its own overlay badge; kicker-above-heading (x6) and blinking-cursor (x6) fire on intentional brief-level devices, though the cursor wraps the real reduced-motion gap.

No user-visible overlay (no interactive browser this session); headless console injection succeeded on all five pages.

## Overall Impression

The site opens at its peak (the hero) and never returns to it. 6,000px of structurally identical sections dissipate momentum; the proof page answers "can I trust this person" with beautifully typeset redaction; every page ends on the least confident moment. Biggest opportunity: the persuasion architecture contradicts stated goals — booking is the least prominent interactive element, and the "I show, not tell" positioning is delivered by a site that shows nothing.

## What's Working

1. The hero earns a senior reader in four sentences; diagnosis before pitch, peer register, typography carrying the authority claimed.
2. The proof discipline is real and rare: gated names, verified boundaries, live source links. The design gives it no visual reward.
3. The newsletter form is engineered with unusual care; its failure state outclasses most sites' success states.
